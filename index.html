Privacy Policy

QuickCalc Mate · by Daynix Studio
Overview

Our Commitment

QuickCalc Mate is an offline, all-in-one calculator app built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection to function.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. QuickCalc Mate does not request, access, or store any of the following:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following information locally on your device only. This data never leaves your phone:

  • Your calculation history (if enabled in Settings)
  • Your theme preference (Light / Dark / System)
  • Your in-app settings

You can clear this data at any time from the app's Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

QuickCalc Mate does not request any special device permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive device feature.

Internet & Third Parties

The app works completely offline. It does not connect to any server or external service. There are no third-party SDKs, no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and no tracking technologies of any kind.

Children's Privacy

QuickCalc Mate does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for use by all age groups.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the app or changes required by law. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
